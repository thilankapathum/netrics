Cell Mapping

Map a previous (replaced) cell to the new cell that replaced it. The new cell will inherit the previous cell's azimuth, beamwidth, multi-beam flag, site, band, carrier and sector. The previous cell will then be excluded from cell search, cell counts, and cell listings, but its historical data is kept as-is.
